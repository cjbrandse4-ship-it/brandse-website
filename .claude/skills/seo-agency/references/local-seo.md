# Local SEO (storefronts and service-area businesses)

## How local ranking works
Google's stated factors: **relevance, distance, prominence.**
Expert consensus (Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors 2026, 47 experts, 187 factors):
- Map pack/Maps: **primary Business Profile category** is the top factor, then proximity to the searcher, then keywords in the business name (only legitimate if it is the real name).
- Whitespark's study of 1.4M+ profiles: names starting with the service keyword win more top-3 positions in 15 of 16 industries. This explains competitors' stuffed names; it does not make stuffing allowed (see `compliance.md`). Report stuffed competitor names through Google's redress form.
- AI visibility (new category in 2026): on-page signals weigh most (~24%), then reviews (~16%), Business Profile (~12%), plus citations and entity signals.
- AI Overviews show on roughly two-thirds of local searches; ranking top 10 organically alone gives only about a one-in-four chance of appearing in one.

## Google Business Profile
**Setup audit (quarterly)**
- Primary category: the most specific true category. Secondary: only real services.
- Service-area businesses: address hidden, service areas set to real cities/regions.
- Services list complete with descriptions; matches the website's service pages.
- Hours (incl. holiday hours), phone, website link with UTM (`utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`), booking/quote link.
- Description: what, where, credentials, languages. No URLs or promo spam.
- Photos: logo, cover, team, vehicles/equipment, real work. Videos: short clips (check Google's current length/size limits).
- Q&A seeded with real customer questions and answered by the owner.
- Attributes (languages, payment, accessibility, women-/veteran-owned if true).

**Weekly routine**
- 1+ new real photo from a job, named for the service and place.
- 1 post (recent job, seasonal advice, offer, event).
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, specific and human.

**Other map platforms:** Apple Business Connect, Bing Places (feeds Copilot and some ChatGPT results). Same data everywhere.

## Reviews program
The strongest controllable local and AI-visibility lever. In a 2026 study of 1,851 local businesses, the ones AI assistants named had about 2.3× more Google reviews than those they didn't.
- Ask **every** customer, the same neutral way, right after the job (SMS + email, direct review link, one reminder).
- Track request coverage: requests sent ÷ jobs completed. Target 100%.
- Track velocity: steady new reviews every month beat a one-time burst.
- Diversify after Google: one or two industry/regional platforms customers already use (e.g. Yelp, Houzz, Angi, HomeStars in Canada, Trustpilot for e-commerce).
- Everything in `compliance.md` → Reviews applies.

## Citations (NAP consistency)
Base layer, not a differentiator. Identical name, address (or hidden address), phone and URL across: Google, Apple, Bing, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, the main national directories, the main industry platforms, chamber of commerce. Fix duplicates and old addresses/phones. Directories also feed AI answers (Yelp in particular showed up heavily in ChatGPT local answers in 2026 studies).

## Service and location pages
- **One page per core service**: what's included, process, pricing guidance, FAQs, real photos, proof (reviews, case), clear call to action.
- **Location pages only where there is real work and substance**: jobs done there, photos taken there, local regulations/permits with official links, local conditions, local FAQs, nearby areas linked. A hub page links all locations. See doorway rules in `compliance.md`.
- **Cost/pricing page** built from real job data (median and range, sample size, date). Among the most-cited local content in AI answers.

## Local links and mentions (by value for effort)
1. Suppliers, manufacturers, partners, distributors.
2. Chamber of commerce, trade associations, certification bodies.
3. Sponsorships: youth sports, events, nonprofits, school programs.
4. Local news and PR: seasonal stories, storm/emergency response, local regulations, community projects (2–4 weeks lead time).
5. Referral partners: realtors, property managers, complementary trades.
6. "Best [service] in [city]" lists and local guides.
Local relevance beats raw domain authority. See `links-and-pr.md`.

## Local AI visibility checklist
- Business Profile complete and active; reviews flowing.
- Listed on the directories AI engines cite in that market.
- On local "best of" lists.
- Website pages answer cost, permits, timing, safety and "who serves [city]" questions in self-contained passages.
- Organization/LocalBusiness schema with `sameAs` to every profile.
