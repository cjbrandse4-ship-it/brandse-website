# Links, digital PR and third-party presence

In 2026 this workstream does double duty: links still feed Google rankings, and third-party mentions are where AI engines find most of the brands they recommend.

## Principles
- Relevance (same industry, same area) beats raw domain authority.
- Earned beats bought. Paid placements must carry `rel="sponsored"` (see `compliance.md`).
- A mention without a link still helps entity recognition and AI recommendations.
- Steady monthly progress beats bursts.

## Tactics by business type
**Local business**
- Supplier/manufacturer "find a dealer/installer" pages.
- Chamber of commerce, trade associations, certification directories.
- Sponsorships and community projects with a local page.
- Local news: seasonal advice, emergencies, regulations, community work.
- Referral partners' resource pages.
- "Best [service] in [city]" lists: find them by searching the query in Google and in ChatGPT/Perplexity, then contact the publishers with proof (reviews, credentials, photos).

**SaaS / B2B**
- Review platforms (G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, vertical marketplaces).
- Roundups and "alternatives to [competitor]" articles: pitch authors with a demo account, pricing, and a customer reference.
- Integration partners' marketplaces and directories.
- Industry associations, conferences, podcasts, newsletters.
- Original research that journalists and bloggers cite (see `saas-seo.md`).
- Founder-led expertise: guest articles, expert quotes for journalists, community answers.

## Digital PR
1. Find a story: original data, a timely local/industry angle, a useful tool or calculator, a strong opinion backed by experience.
2. Package it: headline finding, 3–5 supporting numbers, method, a quote, images.
3. Pitch a short, personal email to relevant journalists/editors; follow up once.
4. Lead time is typically weeks. Track placements, links and mentions.

## Community (Reddit, forums, Facebook groups)
- Use a real, identified account; disclose the affiliation.
- Answer the question fully; link only when it truly helps.
- Never post fake recommendations or coordinate accounts. AI engines and moderators both punish it.

## Tracking
Log every outreach and placement: target, date, contact, status, URL, link type (followed/nofollow/sponsored/mention). Review monthly alongside rankings and the AI prompt panel.
