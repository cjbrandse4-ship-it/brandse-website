# Running an engagement

## 1. Intake checklist
Ask only for what you can't find yourself.

**Business**
- What do you sell, to whom, where? Service area or shipping area. Languages.
- Primary conversion (phone call, quote form, booking, demo, signup, purchase) and its value (average job/deal size, close rate).
- Seasonality, capacity limits (a contractor who is booked out needs different work than one who is idle).
- Top 3–5 competitors the owner actually loses deals to.

**Access**
- Google Search Console (owner or full user), GA4, Google Business Profile manager, Bing Webmaster Tools.
- CMS or code repository and how changes ship.
- CRM / booking data for lead source and real numbers (prices, job counts).
- Brand assets: logo, real photos, credentials, licences, insurance, awards.

**History**
- Past SEO work, agencies, link buying, site migrations, penalties, traffic drops (with dates).

Record everything in the client file (`templates/client-profile.md`).

## 2. Client file
One file per client, kept with the client's project (not in this public skill). It holds: profile, access, KPIs and baseline, what's already done (so nobody redoes it), the prioritized backlog, decisions waiting on the client, and a dated log. Read it at the start of every session and append to the log at the end.

## 3. Prioritization
Score each candidate action:

`priority = impact (1–5) × confidence (1–5) ÷ effort (1–5)`

- **Impact:** how much it can move the primary KPI.
- **Confidence:** strength of evidence (client data > Google documentation > controlled study > vendor study > opinion).
- **Effort:** time, money, client involvement.

Always fix first, regardless of score: anything blocking indexing, policy violations that risk a suspension or penalty, broken conversion paths (forms, phone links, tracking).

## 4. Typical first 90 days
**Local service business**
- Weeks 1–2: tracking (calls, forms, GBP UTM), GBP audit and fixes, review request process for every customer, NAP clean-up on the top directories, technical fixes.
- Weeks 3–6: service pages and city pages with real proof, price/cost page from real jobs, FAQ content, schema.
- Weeks 7–12: local links and PR, video, "best of" list outreach, first AI prompt panel comparison.

**SaaS / B2B**
- Weeks 1–2: tracking (demo/signup attribution, AI referrals), technical audit, entity basics (Organization schema, `sameAs`, consistent naming).
- Weeks 3–8: comparison/alternative pages for every real competitor, pricing page, use-case and integration pages, review-site listings and first customer reviews.
- Weeks 9–12: listicle and roundup outreach, original data piece, case studies, community presence.

## 5. Deliverables
- **Audit:** `templates/audit-report.md` — executive summary, findings with evidence/impact/fix, 30-60-90 plan.
- **Content brief:** `templates/content-brief.md` per page.
- **Monthly report:** `templates/monthly-report.md`.
- **Proposal / scope:** `templates/proposal.md`.

## 6. Communication rules
- Lead with the outcome and the number. Plain language; define any term the owner might not know.
- Separate facts (with source) from estimates and opinions.
- Never promise rankings or AI citations. Promise the work, the measurement and honest reporting.
- Every report ends with decisions the client needs to make.
